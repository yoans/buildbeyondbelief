Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 15, 2026

Build Beyond Belief is operated by Sagaciasoft LLC ("we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

What We Collect

  • Information you give us: Name, email address, and any details you share when you sign up for our newsletter, download the Builder's Blueprint, book a call, or register for a session.
  • Usage data: We use Google Analytics and our own server-side analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This includes pages viewed, time on site, referral source, browser type, and general interaction patterns (such as scroll depth, button clicks, and form engagement). We do not collect your raw IP address. IP addresses are irreversibly hashed before storage and cannot be used to identify you. This data helps us improve the site and is never shared with third parties.
  • Cookies: Google Analytics sets cookies to distinguish users. Our own analytics do not use cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver the Builder's Blueprint and other resources you request.
  • To send you email updates and content you subscribed to. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. You can opt out at any time.
  • To schedule and deliver coaching sessions, workshops, and consultations.
  • To understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not share your email address with anyone outside Sagaciasoft LLC.
  • We do not use your data for purposes other than what's described here.

Email Communications

When you sign up for the Builder's Blueprint or our newsletter, you'll receive emails from us. You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email. We honor all unsubscribe requests promptly.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • Google Analytics for anonymous site analytics.
  • Resend for transactional email, newsletter delivery, and subscriber management.
  • Beehiiv for email newsletter delivery and subscriber management if that provider is configured.
  • Cal.com for scheduling calls and sessions.
  • Stripe for payment processing (we never see or store your full card number).

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing nathaniel@buildbeyondbelief.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
